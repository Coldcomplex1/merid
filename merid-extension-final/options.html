    AI context check

    After Merid replaces a word, Google Gemini checks that it really fits the sentence it landed in. Words that do not fit are swapped for a better one, or changed back on their own. Included - no API key needed. There is a daily limit per person so the service stays available to everyone; signing in raises it.

    Prefer your own key? Paste a free key from aistudio.google.com and Merid will use it instead - requests go straight from this browser to Google, with no daily limit from us. Step-by-step guide ↗

    Data & privacy

    • Page text is scanned locally in your browser to find vocabulary matches.
    • By default the extension makes no network requests - page content stays in your browser and no AI API is used until you opt in below.
    • With the AI context check on, short pieces of the sentence around each replaced word are sent to Google Gemini - by default through merid.site/api/check, which calls Gemini with Merid's keys and keeps only a per-day counter so the daily limit can be enforced. No page addresses, titles or browsing history are sent. Turn the check off above and nothing is sent at all. If you save your own API key, requests go straight from this browser to Google instead and never reach a Merid server.
    • To count the daily limit without requiring an account, Merid creates an anonymous Firebase account for this device on first use. It holds no personal information. Signing in replaces it and raises the limit.
    • If you sign in to the optional deck sync, your email address and your deck (saved words + known words) are stored in your own Merid account (Google Firebase) so you can study on merid.site. Signing out stops all syncing.
    • Merid keeps a personalization profile on this device: counts of how often each word was shown, opened, rated, saved or skipped, plus the word's level and a coarse subject area from the page address. It holds no page text, no titles, no full URLs and no browsing history. If you sign in it is backed up to your own account so your preferences follow you. Read it back - and erase it on its own - under What Merid has learned about you above.
    • Your settings (selected dataset, display mode, intensity, on/off, paused sites) are stored on your device via chrome.storage.
    • Custom vocabulary datasets you upload are validated and stored only on this device - they are never uploaded to Merid, Firebase, or any AI service, and are not part of deck sync.
    • Turn the extension off any time from the popup - globally, or just for one site.
